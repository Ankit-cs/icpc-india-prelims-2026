Unofficial dashboard built from the tentative ranklist released by ICPC India. Tied teams share a rank. The institute topper tag marks the best-ranked team from each college. College names that differed only in spelling or capitalisation were merged. Institute type is read from the college name: IIT, NIT or IIIT, everything else is Other. Regional tags match on team name and college, so a college spelled differently in the two lists can be missed, and two teams with the same name at one college are both tagged.
